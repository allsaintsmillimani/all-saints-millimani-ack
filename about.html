About Us

WHO WE ARE

A Community of Faith, Hope and Love

All Saints Millimani ACK is a Christian community committed to worshipping God, growing together in faith and serving people through the love of Jesus Christ.

We believe that the church is more than a building. It is a family of believers who come together to worship, pray, learn God's Word and serve the community.

Our desire is to see lives transformed through the Gospel and to raise people who live out their faith every day.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered church that transforms lives and positively impacts the community through the Gospel.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurture believers in faith, build strong Christian families and serve our community with love.

OUR FAITH

What We Believe

God

We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Lord and Savior.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the Holy Spirit who guides, strengthens and transforms believers.

The Bible

We believe the Holy Scriptures are God's Word and our guide for faith and life.

Salvation

We believe salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ.

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship God and serve others.
